# Business Sales Performance Analytics – Task 1

## Internship Program
Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics (2026)

## Objective
Analyze business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-selling products, high-value categories, and regional performance to support business growth.

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle)

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel

## Analysis Performed
- Sales trend analysis (monthly)
- Product performance analysis
- Category-wise revenue analysis
- Regional profit analysis
- Segment-wise sales analysis

## Key Insights
- Technology category generates the highest revenue.
- Sales peak in March and October.
- West region is the most profitable.
- Central region shows low profitability.
- Consumer segment contributes the maximum sales.

## Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing categories and products.
- Strengthen strategy in low-profit regions.
- Optimize pricing and discount strategy.
- Increase stock for top-selling products.
- Run targeted campaigns for consumer segment.
